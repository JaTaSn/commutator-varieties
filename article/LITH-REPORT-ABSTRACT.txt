--- TITLE (ASCII) -----------------------------------------------------------

The coordinate ring of the k-fold iterated commutator locus for 2 x 2 matrices

  If "x" between the twos is itself a problem, use:
  The coordinate ring of the k-fold iterated commutator locus for 2-by-2 matrices


--- ABSTRACT (preferred version, about 200 words) ---------------------------------

Take k matrices of size two by two and form their iterated commutator: the
commutator of the first two matrices, then the commutator of that result with
the third matrix, and so on. Asking when this vanishes defines an algebraic
set, and this report is a study of its coordinate ring, over any field in
which 2 is invertible.

The main structural observation is that the defining ideal, although given by a
recursion in k, is in fact determinantal: it is generated by the three two by
two minors of a single explicit two by three matrix of polynomials. From that
presentation the whole structure follows, uniformly in k rather than case by
case. The ideal has codimension two and exactly three minimal generators, the
quotient ring is Cohen-Macaulay, and the ideal is radical. The variety has
exactly k-1 irreducible components, each of them rational, which gives an
irredundant decomposition of the ideal into k-1 prime ideals.

For k equal to two the object is the classical commuting variety of two by two
matrices. For larger k the family is new, and the questions answered here in
full are ones that remain open for commuting varieties of larger matrices.


--- ABSTRACT (short version, about 100 words, if the field is cramped) --------------

Take k matrices of size two by two and form their iterated commutator: the
commutator of the first two, then of that result with the third, and so on.
Its vanishing defines an algebraic set, whose coordinate ring is studied here
over any field in which 2 is invertible. The defining ideal turns out to be
determinantal, generated by the three two by two minors of an explicit two by
three matrix. Consequently, and uniformly in k, it has codimension two and
three minimal generators, the quotient ring is Cohen-Macaulay, the ideal is
radical, and the variety has k-1 rational irreducible components.
