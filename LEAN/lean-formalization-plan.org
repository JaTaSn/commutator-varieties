* Calibration: the one prior data point, and why it doesn't transfer directly

=lattice-line-covers='s Lean formalization (this workspace's only prior one — see its logs) completed 5 named
lemmas/pieces of a real paper *in one day* (2026-08-02), heavily subagent-delegated with hand-worked
proof plans. But that project was explicitly scoped in advance as "fully elementary/constructive, no
exotic machinery needed" — pure number theory (coprimality, Cramer's rule) and real analysis
(arctan-Lipschitz bounds), both extremely well-trodden mathlib territory. *This project needs real
algebraic-geometry/commutative-algebra infrastructure that the searches below show is visibly less
mathlib-mature.* The same one-day pace should not be assumed; it is not a comparable case.

* Live mathlib reconnaissance (this session)

** Solid support confirmed
- Krull dimension and prime height: =ringKrullDim=, =Ideal.height=, Krull's height theorem
  (=Mathlib.RingTheory.Ideal.KrullsHeightTheorem=), =IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal_primeHeight_eq_ringKrullDim=.
- Regular sequences with a real dimension-drop theorem:
  =ringKrullDim_add_length_eq_ringKrullDim_of_isRegular= (=Mathlib.RingTheory.KrullDimension.Regular=).
- Primary decomposition / Lasker–Noether: =Mathlib.RingTheory.Lasker=, =Submodule.IsPrimary=,
  =Submodule.IsMinimalPrimaryDecomposition=, with real supporting lemmas (associated-primes
  correspondence, injectivity, distinctness) — reasonably developed, not just a bare definition.
- Nakayama's lemma: =Mathlib.RingTheory.Nakayama=, several forms.
- Cotangent space machinery: =IsLocalRing.CotangentSpace=, with dimension/span criteria
  (=finrank_cotangentSpace_le_one_iff=, =span_image_eq_top_iff=) — directly relevant to the Jacobian
  criterion (Lemma 17–18 in the article), better news than expected going in.
- General local-property framework for reducedness: =isReduced_ofLocalizationMaximal=,
  =isReduced_localizationPreserves= (=Mathlib.RingTheory.LocalProperties.Reduced=) — infrastructure
  a Serre's-criterion formalization would plug into, though not the criterion itself.

** Confirmed absent (not just "no hits" — checked by exact-name search too)
- =CohenMacaulay= / =IsCohenMacaulay=: zero hits under any search, including a direct
  =loogle "CohenMacaulay"= substring search. No named class exists.
- =minors= as a determinantal-ideal concept: the only hits are Lean's own internal
  meta-programming (recursor "minor premises") and a characteristic-polynomial coefficient
  identity — *nothing* about the ideal generated by the $t times t$ minors of a matrix. No
  determinantal-ideal infrastructure exists at all.
- Serre's criterion ($R_0+S_1 Rightarrow$ reduced) by name: no hits.

* Recommendation

Start with **Theorem 6 (3 minimal generators)** as a bounded trial: smallest dependency footprint
of anything in the article, and a real signal on formalization pace for this project's specific
style of argument (explicit polynomial identities, linear independence, small inductions).

The originally-planned "reconnaissance spike" on irreducibility of the image closure is no longer
needed as a separate step — see =topological-irreducibility-of-image.org=: that fact turned out to
be general-purpose topology already present in mathlib (=IsIrreducible.image=/=.closure=), not the
open question it was first assessed as. The geometric block (Lemmas 7–12) is correspondingly more
promising than this note's first pass suggested. **Serre's criterion (Lemma 18/Theorem 25) is now
the one genuinely open unknown** worth its own short reconnaissance spike before committing real
effort to that block.

**No commitment to the full formalization is implied by this note.** If Jan wants to proceed, the
natural next step is a real Lean project for this once the Theorem 6 trial and a Serre's-criterion
spike have given real signal — not before. Whether that lives as its own sibling project or folded
into =fast-commutators/= directly is worth deciding fresh at that point rather than assumed: the
workspace's one precedent, =lattice-line-covers='s Lean formalization, was originally kept separate
for build-cache reasons and later folded back into its parent project once finished (2026-08-16),
so it no longer argues one way or the other on its own.
